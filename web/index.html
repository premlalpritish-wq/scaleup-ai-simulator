Continuous 40-Hour Unit Operations Train

  • 0. Dry Kelp Milling & Weighing (0–2h): 15.000 g whole dried fronds milled to 0.5–2.0 mm flakes with analytical LCD feedback.
  • 1. Acid Demineralisation Wash (2–16h): 4% citric acid wash protonating alginate into alginic acid while leaching polyphenols.
  • 2. Alkaline Solubilisation (16–32h): 2% Na₂CO₃ at 60°C dissolving 75% solid kelp into viscous amber sodium alginate hydrogel.
  • 3. Centrifugation & Phase Separation (32–34h): 4500 rpm sedimentation isolating waste pellet while supernatant pH is adjusted to 7.0.
  • 4. Alcohol Precipitation (34–36h): Isopropanol/ethanol flocculation generating pure precipitated fibrous alginate curd.
  • 5. Wet-Spinning & Mechanical Pull (36–40h): 2–5% CaCl₂ bath crosslinking into continuous yarn extruded onto motorized spools.
